Choose a side, or mix both in one book.

Cast Spells from range: Fireball, Ice Wall, Meteor, Raise Dead, Mirror Image, Spell Cannon and more.

✊ Sorcerers

Fight with Techniques up close: Dissection slashes, Rushdown combos, the Sanctum dome, Titan Shell and Copy Cast.

How it plays

Every round you draft, every duel you adapt.

1. Draft

Take abilities each round and stack upgrades on them. 41 abilities, 230 upgrades.

2. Plan

Set your cast order, what each ability targets, and how close you fight.

3. Duel

Your fighter casts on their own. Spells clash mid-air, beams collide, and every build has an answer.

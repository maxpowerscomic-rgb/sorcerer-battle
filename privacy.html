Privacy Policy

Wizards vs Sorcerers · Last updated November 25, 2026

Wizards vs Sorcerers does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no login, no analytics and no advertising.

Your game progress (trophies, unlocked cards, cosmetics) is saved only on your device and is deleted if you delete the app.

In-app purchases (gem packs and the Season Pass) are processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Restoring a Season Pass asks Apple for your existing purchases; nothing is sent to us.

Reminders are optional local notifications. If you switch them on, the game asks iOS for permission and schedules them on your device. No push server is involved and nothing about you leaves your device.

If you use Send feedback, the game opens your own mail app with a message for us. Nothing is sent unless you press Send there. If you leave "Include game details" ticked, the message includes the game version, your level, trophies, current hand and device type, so we can reproduce problems. We use feedback only to fix and improve the game.

All opponents are computer-controlled. The game does not connect you with other players.

The game is not directed at children under 13 and contains no third-party advertising or tracking.

Questions: see the support page. About the game: Wizards vs Sorcerers.
